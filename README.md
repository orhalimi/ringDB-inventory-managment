# LOTR Inventory Manager

A Chrome extension for players of the **Lord of the Rings: The Card Game** LCG. It connects to your [RingsDB](https://ringsdb.com) account to track which physical card packs you own and flags cards you can't actually play when building or viewing decks.

## What it does

When you open a deck on RingsDB, the extension automatically marks any card you don't have enough physical copies of with a red **(!)** indicator. It accounts for cards already committed to other active decks, so you always know your true availability at a glance.

## Tabs

- **Packs** — Manage your physical card packs. Set how many copies you own and how many are "active" (available for deckbuilding). Import your marked packs directly from RingsDB with one click.
- **Decks** — Sync your RingsDB decks so the extension knows which cards are already in use. Toggle individual decks inactive to exclude them from availability calculations.
- **Cards** — Browse and search the full card pool.
